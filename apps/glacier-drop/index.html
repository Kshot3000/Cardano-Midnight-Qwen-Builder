How the numbers are made

Thaw schedule

Each destination address is assigned a pseudo-random first-unlock day (its jitter stratum) inside the first 90-day window. From that day, the allocation thaws in INCREMENT_COUNT = 4 installments, every INCREMENT_PERIOD = 90 days. The first three installments release floor(total / 4) STAR each; the last releases the remainder — so the four amounts sum exactly to your total, matching the contract: "the last thawing can be a little bit bigger because it includes the remainder."

Amounts are in STAR

NIGHT has 6 decimals: 1 NIGHT = 1,000,000 STAR. The contract only ever handles integer STAR, so all math here is deterministic integer arithmetic — no float drift, fully unit-tested.

Why the first-unlock day is an input

The per-address first-unlock date is derived from the claim signature, which only exists after you claim in the official portal. This checker takes that day as shown in the portal and computes the rest of the schedule from it. The network-wide progress bar, phase states, and the live Cardano tip are independent of your address.
