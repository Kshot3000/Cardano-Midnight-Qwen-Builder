🧠 About this dashboard

Data: public CoinGecko API (midnight-3) — /coins/midnight-3 and /coins/midnight-3/market_chart?days=7. The market health score is a transparent rubric (complete data, momentum, tradable turnover, 7d history + stability, supply sanity, policy cross-check), computed in nightmarket.js and covered by 26 unit tests. Volatility is the standard deviation of 1-step percentage returns. If an endpoint stops reporting a field, this page shows – rather than inventing a number.

This is an informational tool, not financial advice. Prices and market data come from a third-party aggregator — verify against the source before relying on them.
