No private data — just the public chain ledger.

🧠 About this dashboard

Data: the official data.cardano.org Koios endpoints — drep_list (the registered DRep census), drep_epoch_summary (per-epoch delegated stake + active DRep count, every Conway epoch), totals (per-epoch circulation), and tip. Each drep1… credential is decoded in codec.js per CIP-129 — a 1-byte credential header (key 0x22 / script 0x23) plus a 28-byte blake2b-224 hash — and every identity, census tally, growth math, and health score is re-computed in trend.js and covered by 39 unit tests, including the CIP-129 official test vector and a verbatim real-mainnet fixture. If the indexer stops exposing a field, this page shows – rather than inventing a number.

This is an informational tool over public chain data — verify anything security-critical (especially a DRep you're about to delegate to) against official Cardano sources before relying on it.
