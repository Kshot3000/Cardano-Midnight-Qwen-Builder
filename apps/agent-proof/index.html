🔗 Proof commitments

Every released milestone's proof hash must match the commitment published when the escrow was created — tamper with either side and the mismatch is flagged, not averaged away.

proof_match

🛡 Separation of duties

Only registered non-agent approvers can release or reject a milestone. If the agent approves its own work, or an unregistered party signs off, milestone_state and audit_actors both fail.

milestone_state

⚖️ The accounting identity

funded = released + refunded and the released total must equal the sum of released milestones. The escrow balance is re-derived from the audit events, not the header — so a doctored header can't hide where the money went.

accounting

🧾 Tamper-evident trail

audit_chain demands exactly the sequence 1..N with monotonic time, and audit_actors re-validates every event's actor, state transition, and payload — a rewritten release is caught by name.
