How the numbers are made

Reward pot (per epoch)

P = (fees + ρ · reserves) · (1 − τ) — the live treasury fees plus the reserve decay (ρ = monetaryExpansion) are pooled, then the treasury cut (τ = treasuryCut) is removed. All three values are read from /totals and /cli_protocol_params at the last completed epoch.

Per-pool reward (CIP-16)

Each pool earns P/(1+a0) · (σ′ + s′·a0·((σ′ − s′·(z0−σ′)/z0))/z0) · perf where σ = stake/S, σ′ = min(σ, z0), s′ = min(pledge/S, z0) and z0 = 1/k. The perf (performance) factor is assumed optimal (1.0) for every pool. This is the exact formula in the Cardano Foundation calculator.

Net delegator yield

The pool's gross reward pays the operator's fixed cost first, then the operator's margin; the remainder is split pro-rata across staked ADA. APY = (net per ADA) × 73 epochs. A pool is sat when its stake reaches S/k (optimal size) — past that, per-ADA yield falls.
