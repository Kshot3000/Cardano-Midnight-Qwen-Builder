🧠 Why Cardano is the settlement layer

Cardano's low, predictable fees and deterministic execution make it the practical settlement rail for verifiable workloads: agent escrow, milestone payments, and proof-anchored receipts that Midnight can selectively disclose. When an AI agent finishes a job, someone has to be paid in a cheap, auditable way — that's ADA. Midnight then adds the privacy: the agent proves the job was done without revealing who did what to whom.
