💸 Reward Flow Watch

Every Cardano epoch, the protocol mints a little new ADA from a fixed pool of headroom — the gap between current supply and the 45 billion hard cap — while fees and rewards flow through the unclaimed reward pot. This dashboard pulls the last 20 completed epochs from the official totals table, measures the real mint, the headroom consumed, the fee income, and the implied reward claims, and re-checks the conservation identity in your browser. No private data — just the public chain ledger.

🪙 Reward pot & fee income

Where the new ADA goes. Each epoch the unclaimed reward pot grows by (mint + fees) and shrinks by delegators claiming their rewards into circulation.

Nominal ρ (monetaryExpansion)–protocol parameter
Why the effective rate (≈0.16%/epoch) isn't ρ (0.3%/epoch): the nominal monetaryExpansion parameter sets the maximum release from the reserve, but the actual mint is gated by the reserve ratio — the fraction of supply that still sits in the headroom pool. As supply approaches the 45B cap, the reserve shrinks and so does the absolute mint. This dashboard measures the effective rate straight from the supply deltas and labels the nominal parameter separately, so the two are never conflated.

🧠 About this dashboard

Data: the official data.cardano.org totals table — one row per completed epoch with supply, reserves, treasury, reward, circulation, fees, and withdrawal fields as lovelace strings. The conservation identity, mint/headroom equivalence, reward-pot plausibility, and rate band are all re-computed in flow.js and covered by 21 unit tests, including a real-mainnet fixture of 21 verbatim indexer rows. The window covers the last 20 completed epochs (the current in-progress epoch is excluded). If the indexer stops exposing a field, this page shows – rather than inventing a number.

This is an informational tool over public chain data — verify anything security-critical against official Cardano sources before relying on it.
