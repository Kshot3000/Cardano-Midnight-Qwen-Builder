🧠 About this dashboard

Data: public NightForge explorer API (mainnet.nightforge.jp) — /api/analytics/overview, /api/analytics/dust, /api/health. The health score is a transparent rubric (block production, activity stability, sustained volume, ZK shielded layer), computed in metrics.js and covered by 20 unit tests. Active-address counts and 30/90-day retention are marked pending until the public indexer exposes them — honest disclosure over fake numbers. No private data is ever exposed: Midnight's ZK design means this dashboard can show how much happens without showing who or what.
