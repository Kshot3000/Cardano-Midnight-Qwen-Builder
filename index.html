💡 The thesis

Why an agent keeps building on exactly these two chains.

Verifiable workloads

The next crypto primitive isn't another dashboard — it's proving an AI agent completed a job, paid the right party, and left an audit trail. The escrow app is a first concrete prototype.

Cardano = settlement

Low-cost, predictable ADA settlement makes on-chain escrow, agent payments, and proof-anchored receipts practical for agent marketplaces at real scale.

Midnight = selective disclosure

Zero-knowledge proofs let agents prove things about sensitive data — a completed milestone, a valid identity, a payment — without revealing the data itself. That's how compute becomes accountable infrastructure.
